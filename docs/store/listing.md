# Store listing — HausBoss

**Name:** HausBoss
**Subtitle / short description (≤ 80):** Jobs, crews and customers — run your field service from your phone.

**Full description**

HausBoss is the mobile companion for HausBoss field-service companies. Technicians see their day at a glance and move
jobs forward with one tap; owners and managers dispatch from a live map, reschedule from the calendar and keep every
conversation with customers and crew in one place.

For technicians
- Today: your next job, the rest of the day, and what is already done.
- One-tap flow: Accept → On My Way (the customer gets a text with your ETA) → At Job Site → Clock In → Complete.
- Photos before and after, notes, time tracking and a chat thread per job.
- Works offline: status changes, notes and photos queue up and send when you are back online.
- Share your location with dispatch only while you are on an active job.

For owners and managers
- Calendar by day with every employee's jobs, drag-free rescheduling and the full job hub.
- Dashboard with live technician positions, overdue jobs and one-tap reassignment.
- Customers, estimates (send, accept online), customer text messages and team chat.
- Push notifications for new assignments, status changes, chat and texts.

HausBoss requires an account from a HausBoss company. Background location is used only while a job is en route or in
progress and stops when the job is completed.
